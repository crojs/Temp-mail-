Privacy Policy

Last updated:

At MailAll, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a secure online experience. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our free temporary email service.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

MailAll does not require any personal information to generate a temporary email address. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any other identifying data.

2. Temporary Email Data

Emails received through our service are stored only temporarily and are automatically deleted after a short period. We do not maintain long-term logs of your email content.

3. Cookies and Local Storage

We use minimal local storage to remember your theme preference. No tracking cookies are used.

4. Third-Party Services

We may display advertisements through third-party networks like Google AdSense, which may use cookies as per their policies.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions, contact privacy@mailall.netlify.app.
