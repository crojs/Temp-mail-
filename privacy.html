Privacy Policy

Last updated:

At TempMail Pro, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a secure online experience. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our free temporary email service.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

TempMail Pro does not require any personal information to generate a temporary email address. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any other identifying data. All email addresses are randomly generated and not linked to any individual.

2. Temporary Email Data

Emails received through our service are stored only temporarily to allow you to view them. They are automatically deleted after a short period and are not accessible again. We do not maintain long-term logs of your email content.

3. Cookies and Local Storage

We use minimal local storage to remember your theme preference (dark or light mode). This data is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers. We do not use tracking cookies.

4. Third-Party Services

We may display advertisements through Google AdSense or other third-party advertising networks. These networks may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your browsing history. Please refer to their respective privacy policies for more information.

5. Analytics

We do not use any analytics tools that collect personal information. Any usage statistics, if collected, are aggregated and anonymized.

6. Data Security

We employ reasonable security measures to protect the temporary data stored on our servers. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@tempmailpro.com.
