About MailAll

MailAll was created with a simple mission: to protect your online privacy by providing free, instant temporary email addresses. We believe that everyone should be able to sign up for services without sacrificing their personal inbox.

Why We Built This

In today's digital world, sharing your real email address often leads to spam, phishing attempts, and unwanted marketing. Temporary email services like MailAll act as a shield, allowing you to interact with websites while keeping your personal information private.

Our Features

  • Instant Generation: Get a new email address in seconds.
  • Real Inbox: Receive actual emails and verification codes.
  • Auto Refresh: New emails appear automatically every few seconds.
  • No Registration: No personal details required.
  • Ad-Free Experience: Clean and fast, with minimal ads to support the service.

Our Commitment to Privacy

We do not track your activity or store your personal information. Temporary emails are deleted automatically, ensuring your privacy is always protected.

Who We Serve

MailAll is used by developers, testers, privacy enthusiasts, and everyday internet users who want to avoid spam and keep their real email addresses safe. Whether you need to verify a one-time account or test a signup flow, our service is here to help.

Our Team

We are a small team of privacy advocates and developers who believe in a safer, more open internet. We are constantly improving our service to provide the best user experience possible.

Thank you for choosing MailAll. We hope our service makes your online life easier and more secure.
